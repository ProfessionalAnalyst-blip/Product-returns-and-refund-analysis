# Product-returns-and-refund-analysis

## 1. Project Overview

Welcome to the **Product Returns and Refund Analysis** repository!

This project focuses on identifying the major reasons behind product returns in the e-commerce industry and understanding how returns affect business performance, customer satisfaction, and revenue.

The analysis explores customer purchasing behaviour, payment methods, seasonal sales patterns, product return reasons, and delivery performance to identify opportunities for reducing product returns and improving the overall customer experience.

## 2. Business Problem

Product returns are one of the major challenges faced by e-commerce businesses. High return rates can increase operational costs, reduce profitability, create inventory management challenges, and negatively affect customer satisfaction.

The objective of this project is to analyse historical e-commerce order and return data to identify patterns, understand the root causes of returns, and provide data-driven recommendations to reduce return rates.

## 3. Project Objectives

- Identify the major reasons behind product returns.
- Compare return rates between Cash on Delivery (COD) and online payments.
- Analyse sales trends during festive seasons and promotional periods.
- Identify products and categories with the highest return rates.
- Compare promised delivery dates with actual delivery dates.
- Understand the relationship between delivery delays and product returns.
- Evaluate the financial impact of product returns and refunds.

## 4. Business Questions

### Customer & Payment Analysis
- Which payment method has the highest return rate: COD or online payment?
- Do customers who choose COD return products more frequently?
- Which customer segments contribute most to product returns?

### Sales & Seasonal Analysis
- How do sales change during festive seasons?
- Do festive seasons experience higher return rates?
- Which months generate the highest sales and returns?

### Product & Return Analysis
- Which product categories have the highest return rates?
- What are the most common reasons for product returns?
- Which products generate the highest refund amounts?
- Are certain products consistently associated with customer dissatisfaction?

### Delivery Performance Analysis
- How often are orders delivered later than the promised delivery date?
- Does late delivery increase the probability of a product return?
- Which delivery periods or locations experience the most delays?

## 5. Tools & Technologies

- **SQL Server:** Data extraction, data cleaning, transformation, and analysis.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards, visualizations, and business insights.

## 6. Data Cleaning & Preparation

The raw dataset will be inspected and prepared before analysis.

The cleaning process will focus on:

- Identifying and handling missing values.
- Removing duplicate records.
- Standardizing inconsistent text and date formats.
- Validating order IDs, customer IDs, and product IDs.
- Checking incorrect or inconsistent payment and return statuses.
- Validating promised and actual delivery dates.
- Identifying invalid quantities and transaction amounts.

## 7. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The following KPIs will be used to evaluate business performance:

- Total Orders
- Total Returned Orders
- Overall Return Rate
- Total Sales Revenue
- Total Refund Amount
- Net Revenue After Refunds
- Return Rate by Payment Method
- Average Delivery Delay
- On-Time Delivery Rate
- Product Category Return Rate

## 8. Analysis & Visualizations

The project will include interactive Power BI dashboards covering:

1. **Executive Overview:** Overall sales, orders, returns, refund amounts, and return trends.
2. **Payment Analysis:** Comparison of COD and online payment return rates.
3. **Product Analysis:** Categories and products contributing most to returns.
4. **Seasonal Analysis:** Sales and return patterns across different months and festive periods.
5. **Delivery Analysis:** Promised versus actual delivery performance.

## 9. Key Insights & Business Recommendations

This section will be updated after completing the analysis.

The objective is to translate analytical findings into actionable business recommendations that can help e-commerce businesses:

- Reduce unnecessary product returns.
- Improve delivery performance.
- Optimize payment and refund processes.
- Improve product quality and customer satisfaction.
- Reduce revenue losses associated with returns.
